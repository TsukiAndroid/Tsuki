Tsuki is a free and open-source manga, manhwa, and manhua reader for Android.

Features:
- 1200+ built-in manga sources
- Add custom sources directly in the app
- Standard and Webtoon-optimized reader modes
- Download manga for offline reading
- Favorites, history, and bookmarks
- Search by name, genre, and filters
- Performance mode for older and low-spec devices
- AMOLED dark theme support
- App lock support
- Material 3 interface

Tsuki does not host, store, or distribute manga. Content is loaded from third-party websites through the configured sources.
