Paste any manga site URL and our AI will analyze its structure and generate a ready-to-import .json parser template for Tsuki — no coding needed.

⚠️  AI-generated parsers may need minor tweaks depending on the site's structure. Test it in Tsuki after importing and adjust selectors if needed. Always verify the site is accessible in your region.
⚡
How it works

The AI analyzes the site's HTML structure, detects the CMS family, and maps out the manga list, chapter, and image selectors automatically.

📥
How to import

Download the .json file, open Tsuki, go to Explore → ⋮ menu → Import Parser, or Settings → Manga Sources → Parsers → Import.

🤝
Share your parsers

Got a working parser? Submit it to the Tsuki Parsers repo on GitHub so the whole community can benefit from it!
