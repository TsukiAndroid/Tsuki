Privacy Policy

This policy explains what may stay on your device, what can be sent to other services, and what to consider before enabling optional features in Tsuki.

Effective date: November 22, 2026 · Applies to the Tsuki Android application and this website

1. Overview

Tsuki is a free and open-source Android reader for manga, manhwa, manhua and novels. Reading does not require you to create a Tsuki account. Core library information, such as favorites, reading history and bookmarks, is stored locally by the app on your device.

That does not mean every activity is offline or that no information ever leaves your device. Tsuki connects to third-party content sources and offers optional features and integrations that may transmit information when you use or enable them. The exact information involved depends on the feature, your settings, and the service you choose.

2. Information stored on your device

Depending on the features you use, Tsuki may store app settings and reading-related information locally, including your library, favorites, reading progress, history, bookmarks, downloaded content and other app preferences. This information is used to provide the app's reading and organization features.

Downloaded files and app data may remain on your device until you remove them, clear the app's data, or uninstall Tsuki. Your Android system may also include Tsuki data in device backups according to your device and backup settings.

3. Network connections and third-party content sources

Tsuki needs internet access to retrieve content, source information, parser resources, release information and other online features. When you browse or read from a third-party manga or novel source, your device connects to that provider. The provider may receive your IP address, request details, and technical information normally transmitted by your device or network connection.

Third-party websites control their own data collection, cookies, access rules and privacy practices. Tsuki does not control those providers. Review the relevant provider's terms and privacy policy before using a source. Tsuki does not host or distribute the manga or novel content retrieved from those websites.

The app may also retrieve public project resources from services such as GitHub. Those services process requests under their own policies.

4. Crash reporting

Tsuki includes Sentry crash-reporting support. In the inspected app implementation, crash reporting is opt-in and is disabled by default. If you enable it, diagnostic information about crashes may be sent to Sentry to help identify and fix problems. Crash reports can contain technical details about the app, device and the circumstances of an error.

The Sentry SDK is configured with UI-hierarchy attachment support. For that reason, this policy does not promise that every report is free of screen or interface context. Avoid entering sensitive information into the app when crash reporting is enabled. You can leave crash reporting disabled if you do not want to opt in to this diagnostic sharing.

Tsuki also has local crash-log handling. A locally saved log is not, by that fact alone, automatically uploaded by the local logging handler.

5. Optional account, synchronization and backup features

Some Tsuki functionality is optional and may involve an account, synchronization, or backup service. If you choose to use these features, information needed to provide the selected function may be transmitted to the relevant service. This can include account-related information and the app data you choose to synchronize or back up.

Tsuki also contains an optional Telegram backup integration. Using it means data included in the backup is sent through Telegram or the configured bot/service. Do not use these features for information you do not want handled by the relevant provider. Review the provider's privacy terms and the app's current in-app descriptions before enabling them.

6. Third-party integrations

Tsuki includes integrations with external services such as AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu, Shikimori and Discord. If you connect or use one of these integrations, the information required for that feature may be shared with the relevant provider, for example to authenticate, synchronize or display account-related information.

These providers operate independently of Tsuki and have their own privacy policies, retention practices and account controls. Tsuki cannot control how they process information after it reaches their services.

7. Analytics, advertising and sale of data

This policy does not describe a Tsuki-operated advertising or analytics profile. The app's inspected crash-reporting integration is consent-based as described above. Network requests to third-party sources and integrations are still subject to those providers' own practices.

Tsuki is an open-source project and does not sell your personal information. Because the app and its integrations can change over time, check the source code and release notes for updates to the features you use.

8. Data retention and deletion

Local app data can generally be removed by deleting the relevant items in Tsuki, clearing Tsuki's storage through Android settings, or uninstalling the app. Removing local app data does not necessarily delete copies already sent to a third-party provider, cloud account, or backup destination.

For information stored by Sentry, Telegram, AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu, Shikimori, Discord, GitHub, or a content source, use that provider's own account, privacy or deletion controls. Tsuki cannot directly delete data held by independent third parties.

9. Children's privacy

Tsuki is not designed to collect personal information from children through a Tsuki account registration process. However, third-party content sources may contain material unsuitable for some ages and may apply their own age restrictions. Parents and guardians should supervise use where appropriate.

10. Security

Tsuki uses Android's application environment and available platform protections, but no app, device, network connection or online service can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device and app updated, use device security features, and be careful when connecting external accounts or enabling backups.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Tsuki's code, integrations or features change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy is revised. Continued use of the app after an update means you have had the opportunity to review the revised policy.

12. Contact and source code

Tsuki is an open-source project. You can inspect the implementation or raise a privacy-related question through the GitHub repository and its issue tracker. Please do not post private account details, tokens or other sensitive information in a public issue.

This document describes the app behavior identified in the project's source code and is provided as project information, not as legal advice. App behavior can change between releases. When in doubt, keep optional integrations disabled until you have reviewed their current in-app descriptions and the relevant provider policies.
