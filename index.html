Under the hood

Architecture & Support

How Tsuki is built, and what it runs on.

Kotlin + Jetpack Compose

The app itself is a native Android build on Kotlin and Jetpack Compose — a fork of Yukimi/Futon, extended for long-term stability.

kotatsu-parsers-redo

1200+ built-in sources are powered by the kotatsu-parsers-redo library, kept current so sources keep working as sites change.

Universal Source Beta (USB)

A Gemini AI-powered parser generator, paired with a visual point-and-click rule builder, for sites without a built-in parser.

QuickJS Extension Runtime

JS/Dart/Mihon-style extensions run through QuickJS. Note: some Mihon extensions have known compatibility limits on very old Android 7 devices — this is an upstream constraint, not something Tsuki can fully patch around.

Serverless Parser Generation

The AI parser generator runs on a Vercel serverless function using Gemini 2.5 Flash to turn a site URL into a working parser.

Custom Sources

50+ CMS types are auto-detected for custom source setup, on top of the Specialized Browser for adding any site manually.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Is Tsuki really free?
Yes — Tsuki is free and open source, with no accounts, subscriptions, or paywalls. It always will be.
Why bother supporting Android 6/7 in 2026?
Most manga readers quietly dropped support for older devices. Tsuki was built specifically for people still using them — tested directly on a real Android 7 device rather than just an emulator.
What happens if a source or extension breaks?
This is the core idea behind Tsuki — "app immortality." Between 1200+ built-in sources, auto parser detection, and the Specialized Browser for any URL, the app is designed to keep working even if one piece fails.
Can I use Mihon extensions?
Tsuki supports JS/Dart/Mihon-style extensions through a QuickJS runtime. Some extensions have known compatibility limits on very old Android 7 devices — an upstream constraint, not a Tsuki-specific bug.
Is my reading history private?
Everything is stored locally on your device, and Tsuki includes a built-in app lock so your library stays private.

Your Data

Privacy Policy

The short version. For the complete, legally-worded policy, see the link below.

Everything stays on your device

Your library, reading history, bookmarks, and favorites are stored locally on your device. Tsuki doesn't require an account or login.

App Lock

Tsuki includes a built-in app lock so your library stays private even if someone else picks up your device.

Open Source

The full source code is public on GitHub, so you (or anyone) can verify exactly what the app does with your data.

Third-party sources

Tsuki reads manga from external sites and sources you choose to add. Those sites have their own privacy practices, separate from Tsuki itself.
